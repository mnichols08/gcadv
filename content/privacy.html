Last updated November 5, 2026. This covers garrettcountyadventures.com, including the adventure map and the trail app.

The short version

  • Your GPS location, recorded routes, marked spots and photos stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • Maps, weather and hosting come from outside services. They can see your internet address and a general idea of the area you're looking at, as described below.

Your location

The trail app asks for your phone's location only when you turn on GPS or start recording. Your browser or phone asks your permission first, and you can say no or take it back in your settings. The rest of the app, including the maps and trail lists, still works without it.

Your exact location is used on your phone to show where you are, record your route and guide you to a trail. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, with two exceptions that you control:

  • Conditions. When you open Conditions for where you are, your location is rounded to within about a third of a mile and sent to the National Weather Service and NOAA to get the forecast and snow estimate.
  • Sharing. If you share your position or export a route, it goes wherever you send it.

Phones stop sharing location with web apps when the app is closed or the phone is locked, so the app can't track you in the background.

What's stored on your phone

These are kept in your browser's storage on your device. We can't see them.

  • Routes you record, spots you mark, and checkpoint check-ins, which include where and when.
  • Photos you add, including their location and time if the photo has them.
  • Geocache files you import, and which caches you've marked found.
  • Saved map areas, the last conditions you looked up, and your choices, such as your activity and the town you're staying in.
  • On the adventure map, places you save and GPX files you review. Those files aren't uploaded.

You can delete hikes, spots and photos in the app. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings. That can't be undone, and since we hold no copy, we can't restore it. The same goes if you lose your phone, so export any route you want to keep.

Outside services

Using a map or a live feature means your device connects directly to these services. Each one receives your internet address and basic device information, as any website does, and handles it under its own policy.

  • Netlify hosts this site and receives standard request information for each page. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Mapbox provides the adventure map and the trail app's online 3D view. It receives requests for the map areas you view, plus usage and performance events. It also stores an anonymous ID in your browser, which it uses to count map loads. We checked these events and they don't include your GPS position. See Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • US Geological Survey (USGS) provides the trail app's topographic map, and receives requests for the map areas you view. It also provides river levels. Those requests always cover the whole region, so they say nothing about where you are. See the USGS privacy policy.
  • National Weather Service and NOAA provide alerts, forecasts and snow estimates, and receive a rounded location when you open Conditions. See the National Weather Service privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap's Overpass servers are contacted only if you choose to refresh trails on the adventure map. That request covers a fixed region, not your location.
  • Google Maps opens if you tap Driving directions, and receives the destination. It may also use your location, depending on your Google settings. See Google's privacy policy.

Links to businesses, parks and agencies take you to their own sites, which have their own policies. Business listings aren't paid placements, and we aren't told when you tap one.

Backup

The trail app has no working backup or sign-in right now. If we add one, it will be optional, and we'll update this page first to explain what it stores.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience. We don't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
