GARRETT COUNTY ADVENTURES

Garrett County is more than a place to pass through.

Garrett County Adventures is a local guide to the rolling hills, wooded valleys, small towns, trails, and wild places that make this corner of Maryland worth exploring. Deer, black bears, and other wildlife belong here too: give them room, take the view in, and leave the woods as you found them.
