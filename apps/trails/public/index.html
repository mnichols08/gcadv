    Backup

    Keep your hikes, marked spots, check-ins and photos safe if you lose this phone, and bring them to a new one. It's optional. Everything works without it.

    Backup sends your routes and photo locations to our storage on Cloudflare. They're private to your account and encrypted in storage by Cloudflare, but they aren't end-to-end encrypted, so they aren't hidden from whoever runs this service. Your email is used only to sign you in. Anything already on this phone joins your backup. You can delete it all at any time.

    Backup runs when you have signal and this app is open, so open it for a minute after a hike. Downloaded geocache lists and saved maps aren't included. Photos are kept at the smaller size stored on this phone, so keep your originals.

    Checkpoints

    Real places to reach on foot, with nothing hidden or placed on the ground. Get close and a Check in button appears, even with no signal. Check-ins stay on this phone.

    Stay on trails and follow park rules. Check hours and conditions with the land manager before you go.

    My photos

    Photos are stored only on this phone, at a smaller size. Clearing browser data or uninstalling deletes them, so keep your originals.

      Geocaches

      Load a GPX file of caches you're allowed to use, such as a pocket query from your geocaching account. They stay on this phone and work with no signal. Log your finds on the listing site, too.

        Placing a cache of your own usually needs the land manager's approval. West Virginia State Parks and Maryland's parks and forests both require it.
