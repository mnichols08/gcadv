The files in trails/, hikes.json, routes.json and checkpoints.json are a database derived from:

- OpenStreetMap, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  captured 2026-11-05 (data as of 2026-11-05T23:56:51Z)
- USGS National Digital Trails (public domain), captured 2026-11-05
- USDA Forest Service National Forest System Trails, captured 2026-11-06. The Forest Service
  gives no warranty and disclaims liability for errors and omissions.
- US Census TIGERweb county outlines (public domain)

They are made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/


trail-land.json lists land names from USGS PAD-US (public domain), which gives no warranty. land-rules.json is
our own summary of each land manager's rules, with short quotes. It is a starting point, not legal advice.
